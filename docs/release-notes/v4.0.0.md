Phevere 4.0 turns the notebook into a place to learn. Since 3.0.0 it has gained graded word books, a Learn tab with a quiz and a progress page, saved words that remember where you met them, and one file per word book that moves a book and its progress between devices or to and from Anki. The files on this release were rebuilt from `main` on 2026-11-04, so they also carry the fixes listed at the end.

![Screenshots of the phone app: a Learn question, the Progress page, the word-book library and Families grouped by origin](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/thd2020/phevere/main/README.assets/learning-overview.png)

## Words and Learn

The Notebook tab is now **Words**. Next to My words sit ten graded word books, from Starter 1000 for primary school through 中考, 高考, CET-4, CET-6 and 考研 to TOEIC and Business English, each with a progress ring and a daily goal. A word you learn once counts in every book that lists it. Words → Sources groups your saved words by the book or site you met them in, and any of those groups can become a word book of its own.

Review left Words and became its own tab, **Learn**. It is a quiz instead of flash cards with Again, Hard, Good and Easy: a new word starts with picking its meaning, and as it sticks you pick it by sound, type it, and fill it into a sentence. Answers are graded from whether they were right and how long they took, and scheduling moved to FSRS, the model Anki uses. A right answer is marked on the question with a check drawn like a pen stroke. The Progress page shows your streak, each book's progress, how well you know each word's meaning, spelling and sound, and a forgetting curve for the next month. The daily goal takes any number from 1 to 999.

Families now places most of a notebook by reading each word's origin and Proto-Indo-European root from Wiktionary. A 591-word notebook went from 205 grouped words to 493. Map is a 3D graph you can turn, zoom and search, and Cards open as weekly piles you can scatter, drag or sort. When My words holds words in several languages, chips above the views show one language at a time.

## Saved words

A word saved from the pop-up remembers where you met it: the app, the web page in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, the document and page in a PDF reader, and the sentence around the word. Learn's gap-fill question uses that sentence. Private and incognito windows are never read, and Settings → Capture can turn it off.

Each word book, or My words, exports to one `.pvbook` file with every word, its sentence, its saved date and its whole answer history. Importing it on another desktop or phone rebuilds the progress, and importing the same file twice changes nothing. If a book you made has the same name, Phevere asks whether to replace it or keep both. Export for Anki writes a deck Anki imports as is, and Import reads Anki packages (`.apkg`, including Anki 2.1.50 and later) and Anki text exports. JSON and CSV export are gone; older JSON and CSV files still import. The format is described in [`docs/BOOK_FORMAT.md`](https://github.com/thd2020/phevere/blob/main/docs/BOOK_FORMAT.md).

## Lookup

- Chinese, Russian and Japanese Wiktionary can be switched on in Settings → Sources. They define Chinese, Russian and Japanese words in their own language, and Chinese Wiktionary marks literary senses 〈书〉.
- A plain plural such as "acronyms" opens and is saved as "acronym". A plural with senses of its own, such as "bacteria" or "glasses", keeps its own entry. Words already saved as plain plurals become their singular once.
- Clicking a picture beside a sense shows it larger, with its author, licence and a link to its page on Wikimedia Commons. Pictures arrive far faster and are kept on disk for two weeks.
- Words that no source transcribed, such as holon and operon, now get an IPA from Datamuse.
- Japanese is read by a Japanese voice instead of the Chinese one.

## Desktop

- Quick translation, off until you switch it on in Settings → Shortcuts: Ctrl+Alt+T replaces the selected text in any app with its translation, and Ctrl+Alt+Shift+T opens a panel with each engine's translation to pick from.
- The main window's search field looks words up, and lists matching saved words, recent lookups and settings.
- Settings → API keys has one card per provider with a Test button, and keys are kept across restarts, encrypted with the system keychain.
- A long paragraph read aloud starts after its first sentence, and the neural voices download again after their files changed upstream.
- Installers no longer include the OCR models. Hover lookup over unselectable text and Scan need the OCR pack, a 14 MB download from Settings → Capture or from the Windows setup's Custom options. Upgrading a Windows installation of 3.0 or earlier keeps the models it has.

## Phone

- The back gesture steps back inside the app (closing a sheet or picture, leaving a Learn session, returning through looked-up words and to the tab a lookup came from) before it leaves the app.
- On Cards, tapping the picture turns a card and tapping its text opens the lookup; the meaning side scrolls.
- With a neural voice, each IPA chip says its own pronunciation. Recordings are kept on the phone and fetched ahead, so "Loading pronunciation…" appears only for a slow first download.
- The List view shows every saved word; before, it showed only the 400 changed most recently, and search could not find the rest.

## Fixed since the first 4.0.0 files

Words could freeze the main window, and the phone, when Families was open. The Deep roots count no longer grows every time you come back. The pop-up has no grey band under it on Windows 11, and Settings → Sources switches are kept across restarts. "From" is no longer bold in etymology. The full list is in [`CHANGELOG.md`](https://github.com/thd2020/phevere/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
